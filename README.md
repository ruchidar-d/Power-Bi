# Maven Toys – Power BI Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Power BI sales analytics solution** built for *Maven Toys*, focusing on revenue, profitability, product performance, and location-based insights across multiple years (2017–2018).

The dashboard enables business stakeholders to:

* Track overall sales and profit performance
* Identify high-performing products, categories, and store locations
* Understand seasonal trends and margin behavior
* Derive actionable business recommendations

---

## 🧩 Business Problem

Maven Toys operates across multiple cities, store types, and product categories. While the company generates strong revenue, leadership needs clarity on:

* Which products and categories truly drive profit
* How performance varies by location and store type
* Why sales declined in certain periods despite stable margins
* Where to focus inventory, marketing, and expansion efforts

This Power BI project addresses these questions through interactive visual analytics.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

The analysis is based on transactional sales data containing:

* **Time Dimensions**: Year, Quarter, Month
* **Product Attributes**: Product Name, Product Category
* **Store Attributes**: Store Name, City, Location Type (Downtown, Commercial, Residential, Airport)
* **Financial Metrics**: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Sales Overview:

**Key KPIs**

* Total Sales: **$14.44M**
* Total Profit: **$4.01M**
* Average Profit Margin: **~31%**

**Top Products by Sales**

1. Lego Bricks – $2.4M
2. Colorbuds – $1.6M
3. Magic Sand – $1.0M

**Top Products by Profit**

1. Colorbuds – ~$0.83M
2. Action Figure – ~$0.35M
3. Lego Bricks – ~$0.30M

**Insights**

* Lego Bricks is the *hero product* by revenue
* Colorbuds is the *most profitable product*

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* DAX for calculated measures
* Interactive filters (Year, Quarter, Product, Location)
* Data modeling with fact and dimension tables

---

## 📌 Conclusion

Maven Toys demonstrated strong overall performance, especially in 2017. However, declining sales in later periods highlight the need for:

* Smarter product mix decisions
* Strategic location focus
* Season-aware sales planning

This Power BI dashboard serves as a decision-support tool to drive **data-driven business growth**.
